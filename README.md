## About

I design and build digital products with a focus on clarity, usability and visual polish. My work sits between design and engineering: I plan the experience in Figma, then take it through to a working website.

I'm currently deepening my technical side with low-level and graphics programming.

<br/>

## What I Do

| **Website Development** | Responsive portfolio, landing and marketing sites |
| **Visual & Brand Design** | Graphics, layouts, photo and video editing |
